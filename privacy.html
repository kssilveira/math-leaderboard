Privacy Policy for 1-Minute Math

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 1-Minute Math ("1-Min Math", the web application at https://kssilveira.github.io/math-leaderboard/ and the Android application io.github.kssilveira.twa) handles user and device data.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

1-Minute Math stores the following data locally on your device using browser localStorage:

  • Chosen player display names (pseudonyms/usernames) created on your device
  • Past game scores, per-question accuracy, and response time history used for Practice Mode and Progress charts
  • App preferences such as selected game configuration, dark/light theme, and sound/haptic settings

This local data remains on your device and can be deleted at any time by removing a user profile in the app or clearing your browser/app storage.

2. Information Shared Publicly (Global Leaderboards & Replays)

When you complete a ranked 1-minute game or create a custom game configuration while online:

  • Your chosen display name (username), score, right/wrong answer counts, and the sequence of math questions, answers, and response durations for your best game are transmitted over HTTPS and stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database.
  • This leaderboard and replay data is publicly visible to other players of 1-Minute Math so that global rankings and game replays can be viewed and raced against.
  • If you share a replay link (?replay=...), the replay link itself encodes your chosen display name, score, and question log in the URL.

Please do not use sensitive personal information (such as your full legal name, email address, or phone number) as your leaderboard display name.

3. Personal & Sensitive Device Data We Do Not Collect

1-Minute Math does not collect, access, or request:

  • Account registration, passwords, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Precise or coarse device location data
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or local files
  • Advertising identifiers or behavioral tracking cookies

We do not sell user data to third parties or display third-party behavioral advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following infrastructure providers to operate:

  • GitHub Pages (hosting static application files over HTTPS)
  • Google Firebase Realtime Database (storing public global leaderboard scores and shared game configurations over HTTPS)
  • Vercel (generating dynamic Open Graph preview images when links are shared)

5. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

  • Local device data: You can delete any local player profile at any time using the delete (✕) button next to the username in the app, or by clearing app storage in your device settings.
  • Global leaderboard data: If you would like a public leaderboard entry removed from the Firebase database, please open a request at https://github.com/kssilveira/math-leaderboard/issues or contact the developer via email below with the display name and game configuration to remove.

6. Children's Privacy

1-Minute Math does not knowingly collect personal information from children. All gameplay works without accounts or personal identifiers.
